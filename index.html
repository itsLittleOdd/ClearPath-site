How a project works

Four steps. You stay in charge.

  1. Tell me what's getting stuck.

    Send a short brief from the contact page or book a free 15-minute fit call. Plain words are enough.

  2. We agree on a useful first step.

    A fixed quote for a defined website, a Clarity Session when the scope is unclear, or a support arrangement for what you already use. Scope and fees are agreed in writing before work starts.

  3. You review the work before launch.

    You see the site or the tool working, give consolidated feedback, and nothing goes live until you say so.

  4. You get a clear handoff, with support available if you want it.

    Accounts in your name, a plain-English guide, and 30 days of fixes for defects in the agreed work. Ongoing support is optional and bounded.

Common questions

Straight answers before you write.

Do I have to buy a Clarity Session first?

No. If you already know what you need, a straightforward website goes directly to a fixed quote. The Clarity Session is for when the problem or the scope is still unclear and a written plan would help.

Can you help with the systems we already use without building anything new?

Yes. Workflow Support covers practical guidance, troubleshooting and small improvements for a named set of existing systems. The covered systems, monthly capacity and response targets are written down first.

What do I need to supply for a website?

Final copy, images, logo and any legal text, unless preparing them is quoted separately. Accounts for hosting, domain and connected services are set up in your name.

Is ongoing support required?

No. Website Care and Workflow Support are optional, month to month, and bounded by what is written in the service schedule. No emergency or 24/7 coverage is included.

Do you use AI tools?

Where it earns its keep, and only with information you approve for the job. In the tools I build, drafts wait for a person to review them before anything important goes out. The demos on the work page show that pattern on made-up data.

What happens after I send a brief?

Your email app opens with a draft you can read and change before sending. I read every message myself and reply from the same address with a next step, a quote, or a question.

Question not here? Email JWhalen@ClearPathWV.com. I answer these myself.
