ClearPath demo · Business Brain

Answers that stick to approved sources.

Staff answer the same questions all day: hours, dogs, parking, gift cards. This brain answers only from a small library the owner approved, shows exactly where each answer came from, and says so when it cannot confirm something.

Honest demo: this page uses made-up sample data for The Juniper Table, a fictional cafe. No AI model runs on this page. Answers come from simple matching rules against the sources shown below, right in your browser. It is a demonstration of the workflow and controls ClearPath can build, not a live client system. Nothing is sent or saved.

This interactive demo needs JavaScript.

With JavaScript on, you can ask questions about a fictional cafe and watch every answer cite the approved source it came from. When the sources do not cover a question, the brain says it cannot confirm the answer instead of guessing. It is sample data running in your browser; nothing sends either way.

The idea in three lines:

  • The owner approves a small library of business facts.
  • Answers come only from that library, with the source shown.
  • Anything the library cannot answer goes to the owner, not to a guess.

The approved library

Four short sources the fictional owner approved for this job. The brain may use these and nothing else.

Source 1 · Hours sheet
  • Monday to Thursday: 7:00 am to 3:00 pm
  • Friday and Saturday: 7:00 am to 8:00 pm
  • Sunday: 8:00 am to 2:00 pm
  • Closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Day
Source 2 · House policies
  • Dogs are welcome on the patio only. Service animals are always welcome inside.
  • Parties of 6 or more should call ahead so a table is ready.
  • No reservations for parties under 6. Seating is first come, first served.
Source 3 · Kitchen notes
  • Gluten-free bread is available on request.
  • The soup changes daily. The counter board has today's soup.
  • The espresso machine is serviced the first Tuesday morning of each month. No espresso drinks before 9:00 am that day.
Source 4 · Staff quick answers
  • Gift cards are sold at the register in any amount. Not sold by phone.
  • Lost and found is kept behind the counter for 30 days.
  • Evening guests can park in the gravel lot behind the building after 5:00 pm.

What you just watched

Grounded answers beat confident guesses.

The point is not that a machine can answer. It is that the owner controls what it answers from, and unknowns go to a person instead of a guess.

What the brain did

  • Answered only from the owner-approved library
  • Cited the exact source line behind each answer
  • Said it could not confirm what the sources do not cover
  • Held every draft for approval

What it refused to do

  • Guess at anything outside the approved sources
  • Send an answer on its own
  • Use information the owner never approved
  • Pretend to be a person
