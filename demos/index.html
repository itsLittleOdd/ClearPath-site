ClearPath demos

See ClearPath run the work.

Three small demos built around one fictional cafe, The Juniper Table. Pick one and watch a messy piece of business work become a clear, checkable next step, with the owner holding the final say the whole way.

Honest demo: these demos use made-up sample data for a fictional business. They run entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved, no account is connected, and no live system changes. They show the kind of workflow and controls ClearPath can build, not a live client system.

  1. Requests and follow-up

    Request Desk

    A catering email, a half-finished phone note, and a web form land on the desk. Watch the facts get pulled out, the gaps get flagged, and a reply get drafted that waits for the owner.

    You approve the draft. Nothing is sent.

    Open the Request Desk demo
  2. Answers from approved sources

    Business Brain

    Ask about hours, dogs on the patio, or gift cards. Every answer cites the approved source it came from, and when the sources do not cover a question, it says so instead of guessing.

    You approve the draft reply. No AI model runs on this page.

    Open the Business Brain demo
  3. Site updates with approval

    Website and Operations Manager

    Change the Sunday hours on the cafe site. See the current value, the draft, a live preview, and a plain change summary, then approve and publish.

    The demo publish only updates this page. No real website changes.

    Open the Website Manager demo
