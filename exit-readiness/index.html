What Exit Readiness is

The operations side of getting ready.

Your advisor, CPA and attorney handle the money, the taxes and the deal. I handle the part that usually gets left until last: getting the day-to-day written down so a buyer can trust it and a new owner can run it.

You hire me directly, for a flat project fee agreed in writing before work starts. My fee never depends on whether, when or for how much the business sells.

What I handle

  • Writing down how the work actually gets done
  • Mapping who does what, and what only you can do
  • Organizing systems, records and documents in one place

What stays with your advisors

  • Valuation and what the business is worth
  • Tax, legal and deal structure
  • Investment and retirement planning

What you end up with

  • A business that is ready to show
  • Fewer things that only you know
  • A handover plan the next owner can follow
What you get

Eight things a buyer will want to see.

Every project is scoped to your business, so not every owner needs all eight. We agree on the list in the Clarity Session.

  1. SOPs and process maps

    The key workflows written step by step, in plain English, the way your team really does them.

  2. Roles and responsibilities chart

    Who owns what, who backs them up, and where the handoffs happen.

  3. Owner-dependency list

    Everything that still runs through you, with a plan for each one.

  4. Assets, contracts, vendors and customers inventory

    The relationships and agreements the business depends on, listed in one place.

  5. Systems and tools inventory

    Software and accounts, who owns each login, and what each one is used for.

  6. Missing-information checklist

    The gaps a buyer or their team is likely to ask about, so you can close them early.

  7. Organized document folder

    A clean, labeled folder you control, ready to share with your advisors when you choose.

  8. Transition and handover playbook

    A practical guide for the first months under a new owner or successor.

Where it helps

Useful before, during and after a sale.

  • Before a valuation or sale

    Ready to show

    The business is documented before buyers start asking questions, not after.

  • At your planning meetings

    Records ready ahead of time

    Show up to your advisor or CPA with the operations side already organized.

  • After the sale

    A smoother handover

    A written playbook makes the transition period easier on you, the new owner and your team.

  • Not selling yet

    Less riding on you

    Even if a sale is years off, a business that runs with less of you in it is a better business today.

How it works

Four steps. Flat fees. You stay in charge.

  1. Free 15-minute call: "Is your business ready to sell?"

    A focused working session where we scope what your business needs, with examples tailored to how you operate. You get a written plan and a flat project quote.

    We walk through everything together, close any gaps, and you keep all of it.

What Exit Readiness is not.

This is operations work. For everything below, talk to your financial advisor, CPA or attorney. I am happy to work alongside them.

  • Business valuation or appraisal
  • Tax, legal or accounting advice
  • Investment, retirement or financial planning advice
  • Finding buyers, marketing a business for sale or negotiating a deal
  • Fees based on a sale price or tied to a sale closing
  • Holding or managing your money, accounts or securities
Common questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to be selling soon?

No. Some owners start years ahead. A documented business is easier to run today and easier to hand over later, whether that is a sale, a family member or a key employee.

Will you work with my financial advisor, CPA or attorney?

Yes, if you want me to. I stay in my lane, which is operations. You decide what gets shared with whom, and they handle valuation, tax, legal and investment questions.

How is it priced?

Flat fees, agreed in writing before work starts. The readiness call is free, the Clarity Session is $395 and credited to the project, and the project itself is quoted after we scope it together. Nothing I charge depends on a sale.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. The work and the folder are yours, set up in accounts you control. I only use your information for the agreed project.

How much of my time does it take?

Some, because a lot of this lives in your head. Most of the work happens with you and your team over one to two weeks, and I do the writing and organizing.
