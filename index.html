How it works

One step at a time. You stay in control.

Here is how an engagement usually runs, and who does what along the way.

  1. Tell me what is eating your time. Free

    A 15-minute call. You describe the work and the tools your team already uses. We decide together whether a Clarity Session makes sense. [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM: keep the free fit call as the first step?]

  2. We scope it in a Clarity Session, then I send a fixed quote. $395, credited

    One focused session to sort out what is worth fixing. Then I send a written scope with one fixed price: what I will build, what is included, what is not, and how payment works. You decide before any build work begins.

  3. You choose what I am allowed to use.

    Only the business information you are comfortable sharing. We write down exactly what that includes, and you can change it.

  4. We test it beside the way you work now.

    The first task runs quietly next to your current process, so we can compare results before it touches a real customer.

  5. You look it over, then it goes to work.

    You review the results and give the okay. If something is off, we fix it. Only then does the task go into regular use, with a short plain-English guide so your team can run it without me.

  6. After handoff, you choose what happens next. Optional monthly

    Stop there and keep everything. Start another project. Or have me stay with it on a monthly retainer: we check in once a month, I keep things running, check the output, update your guide when something changes, and bring you the next improvement to approve.

Common questions

Straight answers before you book.

Why do I pay for the Clarity Session?

Because it is real work. You get a focused session, a written Clarity Plan you keep, and a scope for your project. It also means the time goes to owners who are serious about fixing something. If you go ahead with the project, the full $395 comes off the price.

Can I get the $395 back?

No. The session is non-refundable because the work is done once we meet and you have your plan. It is credited in full toward your project if you approve the quote within [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM: 30] days. If something comes up before the session, tell me and we will reschedule.

What if I do not like the quote?

Then you do not approve it, and you owe nothing more. You can ask me to adjust the scope to fit a budget, or walk away with your Clarity Plan. The $395 is not refunded, and no build work starts without your written approval.

How much does a project cost?

Most projects land between $1,500 and $3,000 [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM], depending on how much there is to map, build, and test. Your quote is one fixed price, in writing, before you pay for any build work. If the scope changes later, the change gets its own quote first.

Do I have to pay it all upfront?

Smaller projects are paid upfront. Larger jobs are split into milestones, and each milestone is paid before that part of the work starts. The payment schedule is written into your quote. [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM: milestone split and cancellation before work starts]

Is there a monthly fee?

Not unless you want one. After your project is handed off, you can stop, start another project, or add a monthly retainer at $500, $1,500, or $3,000 a month. A retainer is finished work plus upkeep. It is not unlimited AI work, and what is included is written down before the first month.

Will my staff have to learn a complicated new system?

No. The goal is less for your team to do, not more. The work runs behind the scenes, reaches your team as drafts to check, and anything they do touch comes with a short plain-English guide.

Are the demos on this page what I would actually get?

They show the shape of the work: visible steps, approved sources, and owner approval before anything goes out. They run on sample data for a fictional business, right in your browser. Your real setup is built inside your own tools, on your real requests, to a written scope you approve.

What will you have access to?

Only what the agreed job needs, and only what you approve. We write down what you share before work starts, and you can change it. I work inside the tools you already own.

Do I own the work, and what happens if we stop?

The setup lives inside your own accounts and tools, so the working task, your information, and the written guide stay with you. A project ends at handoff, so there is nothing to cancel. If you add a retainer, it is month to month and you can end it with [JUSTIN TO CONFIRM: 30 days'] notice.

Are third-party software costs included?

No. If a task needs paid software, the cost is yours, and it is listed in your quote and approved before anything starts. I will not sign you up for anything without your okay.

AI makes mistakes. How do I know I can trust the output?

You do not have to trust it blindly. Important output is prepared as a draft for a person to check, and the approval rules decide what may go out on its own. When something is uncertain, it waits for you. The Business Brain demo above shows the same idea: when the approved sources do not cover a question, it says so instead of guessing.

Can you handle sensitive or private work?

Anything sensitive is handled only under rules we agree on in writing, and it waits for a person before it goes anywhere. If you would rather keep certain work fully manual, we leave it out of scope.

Why does my receipt say Whalen Ventures LLC?

ClearPath is a Whalen Ventures company. Payments go through Stripe under that name, so that is what appears on checkout, receipts, and your card statement.
