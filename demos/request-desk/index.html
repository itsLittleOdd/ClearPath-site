ClearPath demo · Request Desk

Watch a messy request become a clear next step.

Requests reach real businesses as half-written emails, scribbled phone notes, and web forms. This desk reads one, pulls out the facts, flags what is missing, drafts a reply, and then stops and waits for the owner.

Honest demo: this page uses made-up sample data for The Juniper Table, a fictional cafe. It runs entirely in your browser as a demonstration of the workflow and controls ClearPath can build, not a live client system. Nothing is sent or saved, and no live system changes.

This interactive demo needs JavaScript.

With JavaScript on, you can pick a sample request from a fictional cafe and watch the desk pull out the facts, flag missing information, assign the next action, draft a reply, and wait for owner approval. Nothing sends either way; it is sample data running in your browser.

You can still see the idea without it:

  • A messy request comes in by email, web form, or phone note.
  • The desk turns it into facts, gaps, a next action, and a draft reply.
  • The reply waits for the owner. Nothing important goes out on its own.
